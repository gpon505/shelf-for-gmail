Shelf

Sections & notes for Gmail — zero access to your email.

How it works

  • In the inbox or a label, click ＋ in the toolbar to make a shelf
  • Drag threads onto a shelf (or hover → ☰)
  • Hover → ✎ to stick a note on a thread (or ✎ in the toolbar of an open email)
  • Color notes & shelves via their swatches
  • Notes toolbar: • / 1. lists and ☑ checkboxes you can tick right on the note

Keyboard shortcuts

Alt + NNote on hovered thread
Alt + MMove hovered thread to a shelf
⌘/Ctrl + ⏎Save & close a note
⌘/Ctrl + B / I / UFormat note text
⌘/Ctrl + ⇧ + 8 / 7 / 9Bullets · numbers · checklist in a note
